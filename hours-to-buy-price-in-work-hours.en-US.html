How do I work out how many hours it takes to buy something?

Hours to buy = price ÷ your hourly value. Example: a $129 pair of sneakers at a $25 hourly value is 129 ÷ 25 ≈ 5.2 hours of work.

What if I'm paid a monthly salary, not by the hour?

Set your income once — hourly or monthly. HoursTag works out your real hourly value and uses it for every conversion.

What does HoursTag show after I enter a price?

You instantly see the price as work time, in your own currency, and you can view any purchase as a percentage of your workday.

How does this help with impulse buying?

Tag each purchase Need, Want or Impulse and watch your patterns appear. Spending insights by category come with a clear monthly breakdown.

Can I plan a goal or a wishlist in work hours?

Yes. Track goals and a wishlist in real work hours, with live progress.

Is my income data private?

No account. No sign-up. No tracking. No ads. Everything stays on your device, and you can back up and restore your data whenever you want.
