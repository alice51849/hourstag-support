HoursTag: see every price as hours of your work

1. What it is

HoursTag is an iPhone app (also on Google Play) that converts any price into the work time it takes you to earn it. You set your income once, hourly or monthly, and the app works out your real hourly value. Enter a price and you instantly see it as hours of work.

  • Turn money into time instantly, in your own currency.
  • Tag spending as Need, Want or Impulse and see your patterns.
  • Track goals and a wishlist in real work hours, with live progress.
  • Spending insights by category with a monthly breakdown.
  • See any purchase as a percentage of your workday.
  • Custom categories with your own icon and color; light and dark themes.
  • 44 languages; back up and restore your data whenever you want.

2. Who it's for

  • People who want to stop impulse buying and think twice before paying.
  • Hourly workers and salaried employees who want to know what a purchase costs in work time.
  • Anyone saving for a goal or a wishlist item who wants to see the hours still to go.
  • People who want a private tool: no account, no sign-up, data stays on the device.

3. How pricing works

HoursTag is a one-time paid download. On the US App Store it is $4.99 (read from the App Store on 2026-11-15; local prices differ, check your store page). There is no subscription and no in-app purchase: nothing to renew, no hidden upgrades. No ads and no tracking.

FAQ

What does HoursTag do?

It converts any price into the hours of work it takes you to earn it, based on the hourly or monthly income you enter.

Is HoursTag a subscription?

No. It is a one-time paid download with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no tracking and no ads. Your data stays on your device, and you can back it up and restore it.

Can I tag impulse purchases?

Yes. Each expense can be tagged Need, Want or Impulse, and you can see spending insights by category each month.

Does it work with my currency?

Yes. Conversions use your own currency, and the app is available in 44 languages.

Is it on Android?

Yes. HoursTag is on the App Store and Google Play.
