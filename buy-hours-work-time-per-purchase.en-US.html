The work hours behind a purchase

Work hours for a purchase = price ÷ your real hourly value. If you are paid monthly, HoursTag works out your real hourly value from your monthly income, then uses it for every price you enter.

Step by step in HoursTag

  1. Set your income once, hourly or monthly.
  2. Enter a price and see it instantly as work time.
  3. Tag it Need, Want or Impulse.
  4. View any purchase as a percentage of your workday.
  5. Check spending insights by category, with a clear monthly breakdown.

Goals and a wishlist in work hours

Saving up for something bigger? Track goals and a wishlist in real work hours, with live progress, so you know how much work stands between you and the thing you want.

Private by design

No account. No sign-up. No tracking. No ads. Everything stays on your device, and you can back up and restore your data whenever you want. HoursTag has no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

How do I count the work hours a purchase takes?

Divide the price by your real hourly value. HoursTag does this for every price you enter, in your own currency.

I am paid monthly. Does it still work?

Yes. Set your income once, hourly or monthly, and HoursTag works out your real hourly value.

Can I see a purchase as part of my workday?

Yes. You can view any purchase as a percentage of your workday.

Does it help with impulse buys?

Tag each purchase Need, Want or Impulse and watch your patterns appear in spending insights by category.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account or sign-up, and everything stays on your device.
